Tetra Duel

  • 1Each player holds 3 tetrominoes. Drag one onto the 8×8 board, then your opponent plays and you draw a new piece.
  • 2Fill a row or a column and it disappears. One line scores 10, two at once 30, three 60. Every placed block is worth 1.
  • 3The game goes on as long as pieces can be placed. If you have no piece that fits, your turn is skipped. When nobody can move, the higher score wins.
