Tetra Duel

  • 1Each player holds 3 tetrominoes. Drag one onto the board, then your opponent plays and you draw a new piece.
  • 2Fill a row or a column and it disappears. One line scores 10, two at once 30, three 60, and whoever completes a line plays again. Every placed block is worth 1.
  • 3The first player to reach the goal wins. With no goal, the game goes on until nobody can place a piece, and the higher score wins. If you have no piece that fits, your turn is skipped.
